Information we collect

When you request a ride on this website or by phone, we collect the information you give us so we can provide and manage that ride:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Pickup and drop-off addresses
  • Flight information, when you provide it
  • Other trip details you choose to provide, such as the date and time, the number of passengers, an email address, and notes about the ride

Payment is handled by the payment processor.

How we use information

We use this information to provide and manage rides. That includes scheduling the trip you requested and sending the text messages described below. Booking alerts go to the owner.

Text messages

If you opt in, Midnight Express LKN may send text messages about your ride: booking confirmations, trip reminders, and day-of updates with driver tracking. We may also send an occasional post-ride review request and an occasional check-in to past customers. Booking alerts go to the owner.

The booking form consent text says: “By submitting this request, you agree to receive automated text messages from Midnight Express LKN at the number you provide, only as necessary for your booking — confirmations and updates. No promotional messages. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.”

You opt in by giving your phone number and checking consent on the booking form, or when you book by phone. Message frequency varies by booking. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. For help, call 980-422-9125. See our Terms and Conditions for the full SMS terms.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Selling of information

We do not sell your personal information.

Updating or deleting your information

To update or delete your information, contact us and tell us what you want changed or removed. Call 980-422-9125 or write to Midnight Express LKN Inc., 7740 Marlette Ln, Sherrills Ford, NC 28673.
